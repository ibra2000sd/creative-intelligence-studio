plain · warm · specific

What sets it apart

The category speaks either like industrial equipment or like a gadget. Haldora speaks like a household: it describes energy as a day and uses warm, editorial design where competitors use dark dashboards or white gloss.

Direction: Daylight Ledger

Homeowners think about energy in terms of their day, not in kilowatts. A warm editorial language separates Haldora from both the industrial and the gadget ends of its category, and the day arc gives every deliverable one shared idea.

Signature: The day arc: a sun on a dashed arc above a horizon, positioned by the time of day. It is the logo, the scroll story, the chart, the app dial and the light on the product.

Voice rules

  • Say what happens in the home, then the number.
  • Use times of day before technical terms.
  • One idea per sentence.

Never

  • Green-on-black dashboards
  • Glossy gadget renders on white
  • Neon glows and lens flares
  • Stock photography of smiling families

Misuse

The day arc

The day arc: a sun on a dashed arc above a horizon, positioned by the time of day. It is the logo, the scroll story, the chart, the app dial and the light on the product.

Rules

  • Construction: the horizon sits at 66% of the format's height; the arc is half an ellipse standing on it, 40% of the width in radius and 50% of the height tall.
  • The sun travels the arc from left (sunrise) to right (sunset). This direction never mirrors, in Arabic or any other language: it depicts the sky, not reading order.
  • One sun per composition. If a picture already contains a sun, nothing else may draw one over it.
  • The arc is dashed in illustration, interface and motion (dash one unit, gap about three). It is solid only in the logo symbol, where dashes would fill in at small sizes.
  • The sun is brand amber in daylight and pale parchment against dawn, dusk and night skies, where amber would disappear.
  • At night there is no sun above the horizon: it is replaced by a smaller pale crescent, or left out.
  • Headline text has right of way: the arc stops at the edges of a text block and stars are kept out of it.

Do not

  • Do not draw two arcs or two suns in one picture.
  • Do not mirror the arc for right-to-left layouts.
  • Do not run the arc through a headline.
  • Do not use the arc as a generic rainbow or a progress bar.

Rules

  • Arabic is set in IBM Plex Sans Arabic throughout. It does not imitate the Latin serif and sans pairing; weight and measure carry the hierarchy instead.
  • Headlines: weight 700, line height 1.25, tracking 0. Statements and quotations: weight 600, one size above body, line height 1.7. Body: weight 400, line height 1.75. Labels: weight 600 at the small size.
  • Never set Arabic words in the monospaced face. It is for figures and units only, which stay in Latin digits and read left to right inside right-to-left text.
  • Arabic text is never letter-spaced and never set in capitals-style small sizes below 14px.

The logo in Arabic contexts

The logo is the Latin wordmark in every market; there is no Arabic lockup. In Arabic layouts the lockup sits at the reading (right) edge, and the name may be written out in Arabic in running text as هالدورا.

Layout

editorial grammar, airy density.

Grid

12 columns, 24px gutters, 96px side margins at full width, content never wider than 1320px. Text blocks span 5 to 7 columns; pictures and data take the rest. On a phone the grid collapses to one column with the margin reduced, and nothing scrolls sideways.

Spacing

Every distance is a multiple of 4px.

Corners

Writing direction

Layouts use start and end, never left and right, so they mirror for Arabic without overrides. Three things do not mirror: the day arc, charts and time controls (time runs left to right), and figures with their units.

Iconography

26 icons on a 24px grid, 1.75px round strokes.

Imagery and motion

Style: Flat layered landscape horizons in brand colour with fine paper grain; the sun sits on a dashed arc

Composition: Low horizon in the lower third, large calm sky, subject placed on the arc

Photography: None at launch. If added later: daylight interiors, no people posed for the camera

Motion: Unhurried, like light moving across a room

Intensity: balanced. Durations (ms): instant 80 · fast 160 · base 280 · slow 520 · cinematic 1100. Spring: stiffness 170, damping 22. With reduced motion: crossfade.
