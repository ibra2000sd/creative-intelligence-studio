Strategy

Haldora is the home battery that explains itself: it shows a household where its energy goes across the day and quietly makes the better choice.

Personality

calm · warm · plain-spoken · quietly clever

Audience

Homeowners with rooftop solar; Existing Haldora customers; Arabic-speaking households in the Gulf

Voice

plain · warm · specific

What sets it apart

The category speaks either like industrial equipment or like a gadget. Haldora speaks like a household: it describes energy as a day and uses warm, editorial design where competitors use dark dashboards or white gloss.

Direction: Daylight Ledger

Homeowners think about energy in terms of their day, not in kilowatts. A warm editorial language separates Haldora from both the industrial and the gadget ends of its category, and the day arc gives every deliverable one shared idea.

Signature: The day arc: a sun on a dashed arc above a horizon, positioned by the time of day. It is the logo, the scroll story, the chart, the app dial and the light on the product.

Voice rules

  • Say what happens in the home, then the number.
  • Use times of day before technical terms.
  • One idea per sentence.

Never

  • Green-on-black dashboards
  • Glossy gadget renders on white
  • Neon glows and lens flares
  • Stock photography of smiling families
