# Thinking in territories

A territory is a position, not a palette. Useful questions:

- What does the audience already believe about this category, and should the brand agree or disagree?
- If the brand were a room, what is it made of and what time of day is it?
- What is the one thing a person should be able to draw from memory afterwards? That is the signature.
- What would this direction make harder? Dark grounds outdoors, serif type at small sizes, heavy motion on old phones.

Layout grammars the DNA understands: `editorial` (type-led, generous margins), `cinematic` (full-bleed scenes), `product-showcase` (object large and alone), `conversion` (short, direct, form-forward), `catalogue` (dense, grid-led), `instrument` (data-forward, tabular).

Motifs the image engine can draw: `arc`, `horizon`, `contour`, `grid`, `orbit`, `strata`, `grain`, `bloom`.

Do not default to any of these. Pick from the brief.
